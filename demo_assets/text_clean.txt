The committee met on Tuesday to review the quarterly figures. Revenue grew by eight percent, driven mostly by the subscription business, while hardware margins stayed flat. Two members asked for a clearer breakdown of regional performance before the next board meeting, and the chair agreed to circulate a revised deck by Friday. Nobody raised the staffing question, although it is likely to come up again once the hiring freeze is reviewed in November. The minutes will be published on the intranet after legal has checked the section on the pending audit.
