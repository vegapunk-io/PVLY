Lab: how a statistical text watermark works

An educational, fully transparent implementation of the green-list scheme (Kirchenbauer et al., 2023) that underlies production text watermarks. A toy "language model" picks each next word from random candidates, preferring the keyed green half. The detector counts green tokens and reports a z-score — no model, no training, no secret beyond the key.

What to notice

  • With the right key the z-score is large (≫ 4) on a few hundred tokens; with a wrong key it collapses to noise — detection is impossible without the key, which is why vendor detectors are closed.
  • Delete or paraphrase half the words: z drops roughly with √(remaining tokens). Short texts (tweets, captions) are undetectable by design.
  • Low-entropy text (code, lists, boilerplate) gives the generator no freedom, so the signal is weak there even in production systems.
  • Production schemes (SynthID-Text tournament sampling, textGrain) hash a longer context and use a distortion-free sampler; the statistics are the same idea.
