Hidden-character scanner & cleaner

Finds zero-width characters, soft hyphens, narrow no-break spaces, bidi controls, homoglyphs and typographic substitutions that get pasted along with text. These are not provider watermarks (keyed statistical watermarks live in word choice and cannot be seen), but they do get flagged by naive detectors and break search, diffs and screen readers.

POST /v1/text/unicode

Why this exists

  • Provider text watermarks (SynthID-Text, OpenAI textGrain, Anthropic's model-level mark) are statistical and keyed — no character-level cleanup touches them, and only the provider can detect them. This tool is hygiene, not evasion.
  • The April 2025 "U+202F in o3 output" story was a sampling artefact, not a watermark; it is still worth normalising because it breaks grep and CMS search.
  • For document pipelines, call the API from your CMS before publishing; the response includes a per-code-point inventory you can log.
