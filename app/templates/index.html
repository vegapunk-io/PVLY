What a report can and cannot say

Strong

A valid Content Credential whose certificate chains to a configured trust anchor, or a vendor verifier confirming its own watermark. Also: proof that signed content was altered afterwards.

Moderate / weak

Valid signature from an unlisted signer; open watermark patterns (copyable); unsigned IPTC/XMP/PNG-text claims (editable); hidden characters in text.

None

Nothing found. Most generators still ship nothing, and messengers, screenshots and social uploads strip what was there. Absence is never evidence of human origin.
