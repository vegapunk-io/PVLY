Honest by design

Two layers. One promise we can keep.

Layer 1 · Removable

Container footprints

C2PA manifests, EXIF/XMP/IPTC, PNG text, ID3 / RIFF / MP4 tags, document authors & revision history.

Removed completely — and verified.

Layer 2 · Reported, never faked

Watermarks inside the content

SynthID, AudioSeal, VideoSeal and similar marks live in the pixels/samples themselves. Metadata removal doesn't touch them.

We tell you. We never claim they're gone.
