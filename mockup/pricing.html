FAQ

Does removal delete embedded watermarks like SynthID?

No — and we will never claim it does. Removal covers Layer-1 container footprints (C2PA, metadata). Embedded marks are detected/reported where possible.

Is removing metadata legal?

Removing metadata from your own files (GPS, device, author) is a normal privacy action. It does not remove legal duties to disclose synthetic content — every output carries its jurisdiction note.

What happens to my files?

Deleted at retention expiry (or immediately in hash-only mode). Reports keep only hashes and findings.
